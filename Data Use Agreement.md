# RatSeizure Dataset Data Use Agreement

## Version 1.0.0

If I am granted access to the RatSeizure dataset:

1. I will use the RatSeizure dataset only for lawful scientific research, education, and related scholarly purposes.

2. I will not use the RatSeizure dataset for any unlawful, harmful, deceptive, or misleading purpose.

3. I will not share access to the RatSeizure dataset with anyone else unless such sharing is expressly authorized by the dataset maintainers.

4. I will exercise all reasonable and prudent care to maintain the physical and electronic security of the RatSeizure dataset and any copies stored on my systems.

5. I will not redistribute, resell, sublicense, publish, or otherwise transfer the RatSeizure dataset, in whole or in part, to any third party without prior written permission from the dataset maintainers.

6. I will exercise all reasonable and prudent care to avoid disclosure of any sensitive, confidential, or improperly included information that may appear in the dataset, metadata, annotations, or related documentation.

7. If I find information within the RatSeizure dataset that I believe is sensitive, confidential, identifying, or improperly included, I will report the location of this information promptly by email to uacvmllab@gmail.com, citing the location of the specific information in question.

8. I understand that the RatSeizure dataset is provided for research purposes only and is not intended for clinical diagnosis, treatment, medical decision-making, or veterinary decision-making.

9. I will indicate the general purpose for which I intend to use the dataset in my application, if such an application is required by the dataset maintainers.

10. If I openly disseminate results derived from the RatSeizure dataset, I will appropriately cite the RatSeizure dataset and associated publication(s) in any paper, thesis, report, preprint, presentation, benchmark, model release, or software release arising from its use.

11. If I openly disseminate my results, I will also contribute the code, evaluation scripts, or other reproducibility materials used to produce those results to a repository that is open to the research community whenever permitted by institutional, legal, and collaborative constraints.

12. This agreement may be terminated by either party at any time, but my obligations with respect to the RatSeizure dataset shall continue after termination.
